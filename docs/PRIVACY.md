# Privacy

OpenRank is local-first. Your workouts belong to you.

## v0.1 guarantees

- **No account.** Nothing to sign up for, nothing to log into.
- **No backend.** There is no server to talk to.
- **No telemetry SDK.** No analytics or advertising SDKs of any kind.
- **No workout upload.** Data never leaves the device unless you export it.
- **Local notifications only.** One-off future notifications scheduled on your
  chosen training days; no remote push infrastructure.

## Your data

- Full backup/restore (`openrank-backup.json`) and CSV exports
  (`workouts.csv`, `bodyweight.csv`, `personal-records.csv`) are free - no
  paywall.
- "Delete all data" removes everything locally.
- The data page offers: Export my data, Backup, Restore, Delete all data.

## Permissions

- Notifications are optional and requested only after an in-app explanation;
  the app remains fully functional when permission is denied.
